## HTC Weather Flip Clock - v1.2.14 (Nov 2026)

**New: weather sounds.** The weather animation on unlock now plays HTC's Sense 4 weather sounds, and shows at full strength like it did on the HTC One X.

### New

- **Weather sounds.** HTC's own sound for the conditions (sunny, clouds, showers, thunder, snow, fog, windy, hot, cold) plays with the unlock animation and fades out with it. It stays quiet when the phone is on silent, vibrate or Do Not Disturb.
- **Weather animation sound setting.** In the widget's settings, just below **Weather animation on unlock**. On by default; tap it to make the animation silent.
- **Full opacity.** The animation now plays at 100% instead of 80%, so it looks the same as in Sense Home.

### Notes

- Android 12+ doesn't let touches pass through another app's window at full opacity, so while the animation is showing, your first touch only fades it out. About 0.05 s later touches reach the home screen again. If you leave it alone it fades by itself after about 6 seconds.
- The weather animation is still optional and off by default; see v1.2.13 for the permissions it needs. On Samsung, keep **Battery** set to **Unrestricted** so it plays right after unlocking.
- The APK is about 36 MB (the sounds add about 2.6 MB).

### Upgrading

Install over v1.2.13 or older; no need to uninstall, and your widget stays on the home screen with your city and settings.
